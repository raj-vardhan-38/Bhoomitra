## Common land-record words (these words are real and commonly used in Bihar)

- Khatiyan: the record of rights from a land survey, showing who holds which plot (khesra) and its area and type.
- Khata number: the account number of a land holder in the record.
- Khesra (plot) number: the number of a specific plot on the survey map.
- Jamabandi: the register of the current land holder in whose name rent (lagan) is collected.
- Dakhil-kharij (mutation): updating the record to the new holder's name after sale, gift, partition or inheritance.
- Lagan / rent receipt: the receipt for land rent paid to the government.
- LPC (Land Possession Certificate): a certificate of possession often needed for loans and schemes.
- Vanshavali: a family tree, used to show heirs.
- Bataidar: a sharecropper who farms someone else's land for a share of the crop.

## SAMPLE RULES FOR THE PROTOTYPE (fictional "Special Land Survey 2026" scheme)

Rule S1. Every land holder in a village under survey must submit a self-declaration form (Prapatra-S1) with plot details within 30 days of the village notice.
Rule S2. Documents to attach: copy of khatiyan or old record, latest rent receipt, Aadhaar of the declarant, and vanshavali if the land is inherited.
Rule S3. If the recorded holder has died, all heirs can apply together with the vanshavali and a death certificate.
Rule S4. Disputes about boundaries must be raised within 15 days of the draft map being displayed in the village (objection form Prapatra-S2).
Rule S5. After the survey, the final record replaces the old khatiyan. Mutation (dakhil-kharij) applications are online through the state portal.
Rule S6. No fee is charged for the self-declaration. Anyone asking for money to "get the name added" should be reported to the Circle Officer.
Rule S7. Help desks (shivir) are held at the panchayat bhawan on notified dates.
